01A little about Mo

My work is about possibility.

My work spans digital payments, mobile money, product management and financial inclusion. Today, I lead product work in digital financial services, building fintech solutions while asking who gets left out when money moves forward.

That question matters beyond the workplace. I mentor, speak and build community because women should be able to grow their wealth, lead with confidence and still make room for all the other parts of their lives.
